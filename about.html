About Prankster News

Prankster News is an independent digital news site covering world, U.S., politics, business, technology, sports, and life and leisure, along with a small opinion section and a few daily games.

The name is a nod to our founder’s sense of humor. The reporting is not a joke. Our news stories are straight news: what happened, why it matters and where the facts come from.

Editorial Standards

  • Two-source verification. Before a news story is published, its key facts are confirmed with at least two independent news outlets or with official records such as court filings, government releases and league results.
  • Our own words. Stories are written in our own words. We do not copy other outlets’ articles, and we do not invent quotes. Quotes come from public statements or from the reporting we cite.
  • Sources listed. Each news story ends with links to the sources we used, so readers can check our work.
  • News and opinion are separate. Commentary is labeled Opinion and kept in its own section.
  • Images labeled. AI-generated images are labeled “AI-generated illustration” and are never presented as photographs of real events. Photos, logos and maps from other sources are credited.

Corrections Policy

We correct mistakes as quickly as we can. If you believe a story contains an error, email tips@pranksternews.com with the story link and the details. We will review it and, when a correction is needed, update the story and add a note at the bottom explaining what changed and when.

Founder
