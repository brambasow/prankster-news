After a 13-Year Wait, 'Grand Theft Auto VI' Arrives With Enormous Hype and Near-Perfect Reviews

Rockstar Games' return to Vice City goes live at midnight local time on Thursday, Nov. 19, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and players in New Zealand were among the first to start playing.

7:30 PM EST November 18, 2026 · 3 min read · by Prankster News Staff

"Grand Theft Auto VI," one of the most anticipated entertainment releases in years, launches Thursday, Nov. 19, ending a 13-year wait since Rockstar Games released "Grand Theft Auto V" in 2013.

Rather than a single worldwide launch, the game becomes playable at midnight local time in each country on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That means players in New Zealand and parts of Asia got in hours before the game's U.S. release, and New Zealand was among the first countries where it went live.

The download is large. Rockstar told players preloading the game that it may need up to 212 gigabytes of storage, depending on the console. The standard edition costs $79.99.

A return to Vice City

The game is set in Leonida, Rockstar's fictionalized version of Florida. Its hub, Vice City, is inspired by Miami, and the wider map includes areas modeled on the Florida Keys and the Everglades.

The story follows Lucia Caminos, the series' first female lead, and Jason Duval, a couple pulled deeper into crime after a job goes wrong. Lucia has recently been released from prison, and Jason is a former soldier who has been working for drug runners. Players can switch between the two.

Rockstar has not revealed the full voice cast. Actress Manni L. Perez was nominated for best performance at The Game Awards for her work in the game.

Near-perfect reviews

Critics have been effusive. As of Wednesday, the review aggregator Metacritic gave the game an average score of 99 out of 100, tying "The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time" for the highest-rated game on the site.

Even before its release, the game led this year's Game Awards nominations with eight, including game of the year. The awards will be presented in December.

Rockstar commissioned an original adaptive score from the Grammy-winning French electronic duo Justice. A 30-track soundtrack album is due Dec. 11, with collaborations involving Tame Impala, SZA, King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard and Gesaffelstein.

Big money on the line

Nearly 16 million copies had been preordered by Tuesday, bringing in about $1.5 billion, according to the market intelligence firm Sensor Tower. Rockstar has not disclosed how much the game cost to make, but analysts estimate it at more than $1 billion.

The expectations are shaped by the game's predecessor. "Grand Theft Auto V" has sold more than 200 million copies since its release. The second trailer for the new game drew more than 475 million views, according to the company.

Miami has embraced its fictional counterpart. Miami-Dade County struck a $3.9 million promotional deal with Rockstar, and a giant "Welcome to Vice City" display sits atop the Miami Heat's arena.

Some fans have gone further, requesting time off from work so they can start playing as soon as the game unlocks.
