In a Rebuke of Trump, Democrats Gain Control of the Senate

Democrats flipped Republican Senate seats in Ohio, the Carolinas, Kansas, Iowa, and Texas.

12:15 AM EST November 4, 2026 · 3 min read · by Prankster News Staff

Democrats took control of the Senate on Tuesday by flipping Republican-held seats in Ohio, North Carolina, South Carolina, Kansas, Iowa, and Texas. Several of those states voted for President Trump by double digits in 2024.

Ohio. Former Senator Sherrod Brown defeated Senator Jon Husted. Husted was appointed in 2025 to the seat JD Vance left when he became vice president. He had been Ohio’s lieutenant governor and secretary of state. Brown, 73, lost his own Senate seat in 2024 to Bernie Moreno and is the first senator since 1988 to return to the chamber after losing reelection.

North Carolina. Former Governor Roy Cooper won the open seat left by retiring Republican Senator Thom Tillis. He defeated Michael Whatley, the former Republican National Committee chairman. Cooper had served two terms as governor.

South Carolina. Pediatrician Annie Andrews defeated Senator Darline Graham. Graham was appointed in July after her brother, longtime Senator Lindsey Graham, died. Andrews is the first Democrat elected to the Senate from South Carolina in nearly three decades. Trump won the state by about 18 points in 2024.

Kansas. Pastor Adam Hamilton defeated Senator Roger Marshall, who was seeking a second term. Marshall, a former obstetrician and House member, had been a reliable supporter of the president. Hamilton, who leads the country’s largest United Methodist congregation, is the first Democrat elected to the Senate from Kansas since George McGill in 1932. McGill lost reelection in 1938, and no Democrat had won the seat since. Trump carried Kansas by more than 16 points in 2024.

Iowa. State Representative Josh Turek defeated Representative Ashley Hinson for the seat Republican Senator Joni Ernst is leaving. Turek, a four-time Paralympian, is the first Democrat Iowa has sent to the Senate since Tom Harkin was reelected in 2008. Hinson, a former television journalist, had said she would be Trump’s top ally in the Senate.

Texas. State Representative James Talarico defeated Attorney General Ken Paxton for the open seat of Senator John Cornyn. Talarico is the first Democrat elected to the Senate from Texas since 1988, more than 30 years ago. The Associated Press called the race with about two-thirds of the vote in, when Talarico led by roughly 59 percent to 39 percent. Trump won Texas by more than 13 points in 2024. Paxton was the attorney general, whom Trump hand-picked for the GOP. His record included an impeachment trial in the Texas Senate and a pending divorce from state Senator Angela Paxton.

Sources: CBS News, NBC News, Associated Press, Bloomberg, Fox News, BBC.
