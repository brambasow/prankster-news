NFL Week 8 Matchups

The last undefeated team, Minnesota, visits Detroit, and Kansas City goes to Denver after its first loss.

2:55 PM EDT October 27, 2026 · 3 min read · by Prankster News Staff

Week 8 is full of division games. Minnesota, the NFL’s last unbeaten team, visits the 4-2 Detroit Lions in an NFC North showdown. Kansas City, coming off its first loss of the season last Sunday night in Seattle, travels to Denver for a key AFC West matchup. Baltimore visits Buffalo in one of the week’s biggest early games, and Philadelphia plays at Washington on Sunday Night Football. The week opens Thursday at Lambeau Field with Carolina at Green Bay and closes Monday night with Chicago at Seattle. The Los Angeles Chargers, still the league’s only winless team at 0-6, cross town to face the Rams.

How Week 8 played out

Updated November 26, 2026, with final results.

Detroit ended Minnesota’s unbeaten start with a 19-15 win, leaving no undefeated teams in the league. Jahmyr Gibbs ran for 153 yards and a touchdown, and Jared Goff’s late touchdown pass to Jameson Williams put the Lions ahead for good.

In Denver, the Broncos rallied from 11 points down in the fourth quarter to beat Kansas City 28-24 and move into first place in the AFC West. Bo Nix threw two touchdown passes, and Denver’s defense held at the goal line on the Chiefs’ final drive. Patrick Mahomes threw for 321 yards in the loss.

Buffalo routed Baltimore 36-10 after Lamar Jackson, back from an ankle injury, hurt his right thumb on the game’s first play. Backup Tyler Huntley was also injured, leaving undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano to finish the game. Cincinnati outlasted Tennessee 43-36 in overtime as Joe Burrow passed for more than 450 yards and five touchdowns and ran for another score, a combination only Norm Van Brocklin had reached before, in 1951.

Tampa Bay beat Atlanta 16-0 in heavy rain without completing a pass, as Baker Mayfield threw only three times and the Buccaneers kept the ball on the ground. Arizona came back to beat Dallas 33-30 in overtime after Gardner Minshew replaced Jacoby Brissett and threw three fourth-quarter touchdown passes. On Monday night, Chicago won 34-17 in Seattle.
