US Stocks Rebound Ahead of Big AI Earnings Week

U.S. stocks rebounded, led by AI shares, ahead of a heavy week of technology earnings.

October 19, 2026 · 3 min read · Summary by Prankster News
Based on reporting by Reuters and the Associated Press

U.S. stocks rebounded on Monday, with AI-related shares leading the recovery after last week’s sharp sell-off. The Nasdaq rose more than 1% as investors looked ahead to a busy week of earnings from major technology companies.

Companies including Meta, Tesla, and others are scheduled to report results in the coming days. Easing oil prices and lower Treasury yields also contributed to the more positive tone in the market.

Sources: Reuters, Associated Press.
