NFL Week 11 Matchups

Week 10 brought blowouts, a shootout and a long-awaited first win.

11:30 PM EST November 16, 2026 · 4 min read · by Prankster News Staff

Week 10 brought blowouts, a shootout and a long-awaited first win. Jahmyr Gibbs piled up 252 total yards as the Detroit Lions routed New England 35-10 in Munich. The win was costly, though. Aidan Hutchinson hurt his knee celebrating the same sack that knocked Drake Maye out with an ankle injury. Joe Burrow threw for 409 yards and four touchdowns, and Ja'Marr Chase caught 11 passes for 231 yards, as Cincinnati beat Pittsburgh 38-18 to take sole possession of first place in the AFC North. Kirk Cousins and Minnesota beat Green Bay 30-10. Christian McCaffrey scored three times as San Francisco survived Dallas 46-40, despite five touchdown passes from Dak Prescott. Patrick Mahomes threw five touchdowns in Kansas City's win at Atlanta, and No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza made his first start in the Raiders' loss to Seattle.

On Monday night, the Los Angeles Chargers beat Baltimore 23-20 for their first win of the season. The loss was the Ravens' fifth straight. The Bills and 49ers are still the only teams with one loss at 8-1, and the Lions, Bears and Vikings are all 7-2 in the NFC North.

Week 11 includes the Mexico City game, where the 49ers, 8-1, are the home team against the Vikings, 7-2, on Sunday night. The Bengals, 7-2, visit the Commanders on Monday night with Burrow on a five-game winning streak. The Lions, 7-2, host the Buccaneers, 3-6, before a Thanksgiving showdown with Chicago. The Jaguars, 7-2, visit the Giants, who still lead the NFC East. The Steelers, 6-3, try to bounce back in Philadelphia against the 2-7 Eagles. Six teams are on bye, the most of any week so far this season.
