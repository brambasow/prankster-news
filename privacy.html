Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Prankster News (“we,” “us,” or “our”) runs pranksternews.com. This policy explains what information is collected when you read the site, who collects it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We do not ask readers to create accounts, and we do not sell personal information. Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical details when a page is requested, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the page you asked for and the time of the request. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

How We Use Information

We use this information to keep the site running and secure, to understand which stories readers find useful, to answer messages and corrections, and to show advertising that helps pay for the site.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. To learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent message from Google asks for your choices before personalized ads or cookies are used. You can change those choices at any time with the “Privacy settings” link at the bottom of every page.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see how the site performs. Cloudflare says this service does not use cookies or local storage to track visitors and does not build profiles of individual readers. Our site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes requests to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for details.

Cookies and Your Choices

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Our own pages do not need cookies to work; the cookies you may see come from our advertising partners described above. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use the opt-out links above, or use the “Privacy settings” link in the footer where it is available.

Links to Other Sites

Our stories link to news outlets, government agencies and other sources. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Children’s Privacy

The site is written for a general adult audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how information is handled, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to tips@pranksternews.com or through our contact page.
